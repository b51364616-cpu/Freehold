What's different about a Freehold site.

Built by hand, not dragged from a template.

Every site starts from a blank page and is designed around your trade. That means things templates can't do, like a warning-light guide, a tile problem finder or a room-by-room checklist, and pages that load fast because there's nothing in them you don't need.

An editor you'll actually use.

Change your prices, your hours, your photos or your reviews from your phone, then press publish. No logins to other platforms and no code. It's live in about a minute.
