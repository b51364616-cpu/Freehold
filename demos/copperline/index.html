Queensland smoke alarm legislation

Every Queensland home needs interconnected alarms by 2027.

Since January 2022, any home being sold, leased, or having a lease renewed has needed photoelectric, interconnected smoke alarms — in every bedroom, in the hallways connecting them, and on every storey. From 1 January 2027 that extends to all owner-occupied homes in the state.

Interconnected means when one sounds, they all sound. It's the single change most likely to get everyone out of a house at 3am, and it's why the legislation exists.

Most three bedroom houses need four to five alarms. We supply and fit a compliant set, interconnect them, test the lot and hand you the paperwork — usually in a morning.
